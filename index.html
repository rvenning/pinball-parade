📖 How to play

  • Flippers: hold the left or right side of the bottom of the table. Both thumbs at once works.
  • Launch: tap the glowing Launch button. Hold it longer to launch harder.
  • Pick any table: three balls to score as many points as you can.
  • Combos: alternate ramps, saucers, completed target banks and lanes within eight seconds for bigger jackpots.
  • Multiball: shoot a lit saucer twice to release a second ball.
  • Parade: keep hitting bumpers to fill the meter — then everything scores double.
  • High scores: every table has its own family leaderboard.
  • Keys: ← → or A D for flippers, Space to launch, P to pause.
